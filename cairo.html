CAIRO

Egypt's capital and one of the world's most historic cities, connecting Africa, the Middle East and the Mediterranean through commerce, culture and innovation.

Why Cairo Matters

Cairo is one of the largest cities in Africa and the Arab world. As Egypt's political, economic and cultural center, it serves as a gateway connecting Africa, Europe and the Middle East.

For centuries, Cairo has influenced trade, education, media, architecture and regional politics. Today it remains a major destination for investors, entrepreneurs, tourists and multinational companies.

A Global Historical Capital

Cairo is home to some of humanity's most famous historical landmarks. The nearby Pyramids of Giza, the Great Sphinx and centuries of Islamic, Coptic and Pharaonic heritage make the city one of the world's most visited cultural destinations.

Its rich history continues to attract researchers, travelers, entrepreneurs and creative industries from around the globe.

Key Industries

Major sectors include tourism, finance, telecommunications, manufacturing, logistics, media, construction, education and technology.

Cairo remains one of the largest consumer markets in Africa and a strategic base for companies serving the Middle East and North Africa.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include the Pyramids of Giza, Egyptian Museum, Grand Egyptian Museum, Khan El Khalili, Cairo Citadel, Al-Azhar Mosque and the Nile Corniche.

The city offers a unique blend of ancient civilization, modern urban life and vibrant cultural experiences.

Future Growth

Egypt continues investing heavily in infrastructure, transportation, technology and urban development projects, including the New Administrative Capital east of Cairo.

These initiatives are strengthening Cairo's position as a major economic and investment hub for the wider region.
