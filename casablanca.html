CASABLANCA

Morocco's economic capital and one of Africa's leading financial, commercial and international business centers.

Why Casablanca Matters

Casablanca is Morocco's largest city and economic powerhouse. Located on the Atlantic coast, it serves as a strategic bridge between Africa, Europe and the Middle East.

The city hosts major financial institutions, multinational corporations, industrial zones and one of Africa's busiest ports, making it one of the continent's most important business destinations.

A Gateway Between Continents

Casablanca occupies a unique position connecting African markets with Europe and the Middle East. This strategic location has helped the city become a major logistics, finance and trade center.

International businesses frequently choose Casablanca as a base for expanding operations throughout North and West Africa.

Key Industries

Major sectors include finance, banking, logistics, manufacturing, automotive production, aerospace, tourism, telecommunications and renewable energy.

Casablanca Finance City continues attracting international companies seeking access to African markets.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Hassan II Mosque, Ain Diab Corniche, Morocco Mall, Old Medina, Habous Quarter and the city's growing cultural and business districts.

Casablanca blends modern business infrastructure with Moroccan culture, architecture and Atlantic coastal living.

Future Growth

Infrastructure modernization, renewable energy projects, port expansion and international investment continue strengthening Casablanca's position as one of Africa's leading economic hubs.

The city remains central to Morocco's ambitions of becoming a major gateway for trade and investment across Africa.
