TBILISI

Georgia's capital city, known for entrepreneurship, affordability, digital nomad communities, tourism and strategic access between Europe and Asia.

Why Tbilisi Matters

Tbilisi has emerged as one of the most dynamic cities in the Caucasus region. Located at the crossroads of Europe and Asia, the city combines a rich cultural heritage with a growing entrepreneurial ecosystem and attractive business environment.

Its affordability, welcoming visa policies and increasing international connectivity continue attracting investors, entrepreneurs, remote workers and travelers from around the world.

A Growing Entrepreneurial Hub

Tbilisi has gained international attention as a startup-friendly destination with relatively simple business registration procedures, competitive costs and strong access to regional markets.

Its growing community of entrepreneurs and freelancers continues supporting innovation across technology, services and creative industries.

Key Industries

Major sectors include tourism, hospitality, technology, financial services, logistics, agriculture, real estate, education and professional services.

Georgia's business-friendly policies continue helping Tbilisi strengthen its position as a regional economic center.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Old Tbilisi, Narikala Fortress, Freedom Square, Rustaveli Avenue, Holy Trinity Cathedral, Mtatsminda Park, Sulfur Baths and the historic districts along the Kura River.

The city offers a distinctive blend of architecture, cuisine, culture and mountain scenery.

Future Outlook

Continued investment in tourism, technology, transportation and international business development is helping Tbilisi attract growing global attention.

Its strategic location between Europe, Central Asia and the Middle East positions the city for long-term growth.
