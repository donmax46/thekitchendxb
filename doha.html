DOHA

The capital of Qatar and one of the Gulf region's most dynamic centers for business, finance, culture and international development.

Why Doha Matters

Doha has transformed from a regional trading port into a modern global city. As Qatar's capital and economic center, it plays a major role in energy markets, finance, aviation, logistics and international diplomacy.

Major investments in infrastructure, tourism, education and technology continue to position Doha as one of the Middle East's most influential cities.

Key Industries

Doha's economy is supported by energy, finance, aviation, logistics, tourism, construction, education and technology.

Qatar continues investing heavily in economic diversification, creating opportunities across multiple sectors beyond oil and gas.

Major Attractions

Visitors enjoy destinations such as Souq Waqif, Museum of Islamic Art, National Museum of Qatar, The Pearl, Katara Cultural Village and Doha Corniche.

The city combines modern architecture, traditional Arabian culture and world-class hospitality, creating a unique destination for residents and visitors alike.

Future Growth

Doha continues expanding through major infrastructure projects, smart city initiatives, tourism investments and international business development.

Its strategic location, strong economy and global connectivity position the city as one of the Gulf region's most important business and lifestyle destinations.
