ABU DHABI

The capital of the United Arab Emirates and one of the Middle East's leading centers for investment, innovation, culture and long-term economic growth.

Why Abu Dhabi Matters

Abu Dhabi is the political and administrative capital of the United Arab Emirates. It is home to major government institutions, sovereign wealth funds, multinational corporations and strategic industries that help shape the region's future.

Known for stability, infrastructure and long-term planning, Abu Dhabi attracts professionals, entrepreneurs, investors and visitors from around the world.

Key Industries

Abu Dhabi's economy is supported by energy, finance, logistics, aviation, tourism, manufacturing, healthcare, education and technology.

Major economic diversification projects continue to position Abu Dhabi as one of the most important investment destinations in the Middle East.

Major Attractions

Visitors to Abu Dhabi enjoy world-famous destinations including Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Louvre Abu Dhabi, Yas Island, Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World, Saadiyat Island and the Abu Dhabi Corniche.

The city blends modern architecture, cultural heritage, luxury hospitality and natural coastal beauty.
