DOUALA

Cameroon's economic capital and largest city, serving as the country's primary commercial, financial and logistics center.

Why Douala Matters

Douala is the economic engine of Cameroon and one of Central Africa's most important commercial hubs. Located on the Wouri River near the Atlantic coast, the city handles a significant portion of regional trade and serves as a gateway for imports and exports across the region.

As Cameroon's largest city, Douala attracts entrepreneurs, investors, multinational companies and professionals seeking opportunities in one of Central Africa's most dynamic markets.

The Economic Heart Of Cameroon

Douala hosts the headquarters of many of Cameroon's largest companies and financial institutions. The city's port infrastructure and commercial networks make it a vital link for trade across Central Africa.

Its strategic coastal location supports business activity extending beyond Cameroon into neighboring countries.

Key Industries

Major sectors include logistics, shipping, banking, telecommunications, construction, manufacturing, retail, energy services and professional services.

The city continues to expand through infrastructure development, industrial growth and regional trade integration.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Bonanjo, Akwa, Bonapriso, the Wouri Bridge, Douala Maritime Museum, local markets and the city's vibrant cultural districts.

Douala offers a unique mix of business energy, cultural diversity and entrepreneurial spirit.

Future Outlook

Investment in transportation, logistics, industrial development and digital infrastructure continues strengthening Douala's position as one of Central Africa's leading economic centers.

Its role as a commercial gateway makes it a key city for future regional growth and investment.
