BAGHDAD

Iraq's capital city and one of the world's most historic centers of knowledge, trade, culture and regional influence.

Why Baghdad Matters

Baghdad has been one of the most influential cities in the Middle East for centuries. During the Islamic Golden Age, it became a global center for science, education, philosophy, literature and trade.

Today, Baghdad remains Iraq's political, economic and cultural capital while continuing to play an important role in the country's development and future growth.

A Historic Center Of Knowledge

Baghdad became famous through institutions such as the House of Wisdom, which attracted scholars from across the known world. The city played a major role in mathematics, astronomy, medicine and philosophy.

Its legacy continues to shape the intellectual and cultural history of the Middle East and beyond.

Key Industries

Major sectors include government services, construction, telecommunications, trade, finance, transportation, education, healthcare and energy-related industries.

Ongoing investment in infrastructure and economic development continues creating new opportunities across the city.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Al-Mutanabbi Street, the National Museum of Iraq, Abbasid Palace, Al-Shaheed Monument, Baghdad Tower and the historic districts along the Tigris River.

The city offers a rich blend of history, culture, architecture and local traditions.

Future Outlook

Baghdad continues investing in infrastructure, transportation, technology and business development aimed at supporting long-term economic growth and urban modernization.

Its strategic location and historical significance position the city as an important center for Iraq's future development.
