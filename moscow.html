MOSCOW

Russia's capital and largest city, serving as a major center for finance, technology, culture, education and international commerce.

Why Moscow Matters

Moscow is one of Europe's largest cities and the economic heart of Russia. The city plays a central role in finance, government, technology, energy, manufacturing and international business.

With its extensive infrastructure, highly educated workforce and strategic position between Europe and Asia, Moscow remains one of the world's most influential metropolitan centers.

A Global Capital

Moscow has long been a center of political influence, economic activity and cultural development. The city hosts major corporations, financial institutions and research organizations serving domestic and international markets.

Its extensive transportation network connects Russia with Europe, Central Asia, the Caucasus and East Asia.

Key Industries

Major sectors include finance, technology, engineering, telecommunications, manufacturing, energy services, logistics, retail, construction and professional services.

Moscow remains the country's largest business center and a key driver of economic activity across the wider region.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Red Square, the Kremlin, Saint Basil's Cathedral, Bolshoi Theatre, Gorky Park, Moscow Metro and Tretyakov Gallery.

The city offers a unique blend of historic architecture, modern development, cultural institutions and vibrant urban life.

Future Outlook

Investment in technology, infrastructure, transportation and research continues shaping Moscow's future as a major Eurasian business and innovation hub.

Its position between European and Asian markets ensures its continued importance in trade, finance and economic development.
