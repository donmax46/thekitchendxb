KARACHI

Pakistan's largest city and economic capital, serving as the country's leading center for finance, trade, logistics and entrepreneurship.

Why Karachi Matters

Karachi is Pakistan's financial and commercial heart, generating a significant share of the country's economic activity. Located on the Arabian Sea, the city hosts major ports, financial institutions, multinational companies and industrial centers.

Its strategic position makes Karachi one of South Asia's most important gateways for international trade and business.

Pakistan's Economic Capital

Karachi is home to the Pakistan Stock Exchange, major banks and some of the country's largest corporations. Its economy spans finance, manufacturing, trade, logistics and technology.

The city remains a key destination for local and international investors seeking access to Pakistan's large consumer market.

Key Industries

Major sectors include finance, logistics, shipping, manufacturing, telecommunications, retail, healthcare, technology, real estate and professional services.

Karachi continues to drive economic growth through innovation, trade and infrastructure development.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Clifton Beach, Mohatta Palace, Quaid-e-Azam Mausoleum, Frere Hall, Port Grand, Dolmen Mall Clifton and the city's vibrant food and cultural districts.

Karachi offers a dynamic mix of history, commerce, culture and coastal living.

Trade & Logistics

Karachi hosts Pakistan's busiest seaports and serves as the country's primary maritime gateway. The city's logistics infrastructure supports regional and international commerce across South Asia and the Middle East.

Its role in trade continues to strengthen its importance as an economic hub.

Future Outlook

Investment in transportation, technology, urban development and logistics is helping position Karachi for continued economic growth.

Its large population, entrepreneurial culture and strategic location support long-term opportunities across multiple industries.
