MEDICAL CANNABIS - DUBAI - AWARENESS

What Should Medical Cannabis Patients Know Before Dubai Travel?

Medical cannabis authorization in another country does not automatically determine what is allowed when traveling to Dubai.

Do Not Assume Transferability

A medical cannabis card, prescription or authorization from another country may not be recognized in the same way in the UAE.

Patients should verify UAE-specific requirements before traveling with any cannabis-related product.

Speak With Qualified Professionals

Patients should consult qualified medical and legal sources before making travel decisions.

If requirements cannot be confirmed clearly, the safest approach is not to travel with the product.

Key Takeaways

  • Medical cannabis rules differ by country.
  • Foreign authorization may not transfer to Dubai.
  • UAE-specific verification is essential.
  • Qualified medical and legal guidance matters.
  • Do not travel with products that cannot be verified.
