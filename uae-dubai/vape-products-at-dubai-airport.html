AIRPORT - VAPING - TRAVEL

Vape Products At Dubai Airport

Travelers should check airline, airport and UAE requirements before carrying vape devices, liquids or cartridges through Dubai.

Before Packing

Review airline and destination rules before packing vape devices or liquids. Requirements can differ between carriers and jurisdictions.

Products with unknown, cannabis-derived or restricted ingredients should not be carried.

Documentation And Labels

Clear commercial labeling may help identify ordinary products, but it does not override local law.

Travelers remain responsible for what they carry.

Avoid Carrying For Others

Do not carry vape liquids, cartridges or devices for someone else if you cannot verify the contents and rules.

Responsible travel means knowing what is in your luggage.

Key Takeaways

  • Check airline, airport and UAE rules before packing vape products.
  • Unknown or restricted ingredients create risk.
  • Labels do not override local law.
  • Travelers are responsible for their luggage.
  • Do not carry vape products casually for others.
