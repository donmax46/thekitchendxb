DUBAI - TRAVEL - SAFETY

Traveler Safety Checklist For Dubai

A calm pre-travel checklist can help Dubai visitors avoid preventable legal, medication and public-safety issues.

Before You Travel

Review UAE laws that may affect medication, controlled substances, vaping products and public conduct.

Keep important documents accessible, including prescriptions, insurance details and emergency contacts.

At The Airport

Carry medication in a clear and documented way. Avoid carrying unknown items for others.

If officials ask questions, remain calm, respectful and truthful.

During Your Stay

Use official or reputable sources when questions arise. Avoid risky assumptions about local laws.

Responsible travel includes respecting local rules and seeking qualified help when needed.

Key Takeaways

  • Check local laws before traveling to Dubai.
  • Keep prescriptions and documents accessible.
  • Do not carry unknown items for others.
  • Use official sources for legal and travel questions.
  • Responsible travel reduces preventable risk.
