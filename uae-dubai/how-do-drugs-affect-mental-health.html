How Do Drugs Affect Mental Health?

Substance misuse can affect mood, judgment, relationships and mental wellbeing, making early support and prevention education important.

Mood And Judgment

Drugs may affect anxiety, mood, attention, impulse control and decision-making. These effects can create safety concerns for the individual and people around them.

People with existing mental-health concerns may face additional risks and should seek qualified support.

Dependence And Stress

Some substances can contribute to dependence, withdrawal symptoms or cycles of stress and avoidance.

Supportive conversations can help people take concerns seriously without turning the issue into blame or stigma.

When To Seek Help

People should seek professional help if substance use is affecting sleep, work, school, relationships, safety or emotional stability.

Emergency services should be contacted when there is immediate danger, severe distress or a medical emergency.

Key Takeaways

  • Substance misuse can affect mood, anxiety and decision-making.
  • Mental-health concerns can increase vulnerability.
  • Dependence and withdrawal require qualified support.
  • Early help can reduce harm.
  • Emergency situations require immediate professional assistance.
