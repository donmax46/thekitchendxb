Airport Medication Rules In Dubai

Travelers carrying medication through Dubai should verify requirements, keep documentation and avoid assumptions about controlled medicines.

Check Before You Fly

Some medicines may be controlled or require documentation in the UAE. Travelers should check official requirements before flying.

A medicine that is common in one country may still require extra care when entering another jurisdiction.

Keep Documentation Clear

Travelers should keep prescriptions, medical letters and original packaging where appropriate. Documents should match the traveler's name and current medical need.

Carrying only a reasonable personal quantity can also reduce confusion.

Use Official Guidance

Airlines, official UAE sources and qualified medical professionals can help travelers understand what documentation may be needed.

If a medicine cannot be verified, travelers should resolve the question before departure.

Key Takeaways

  • Medication rules should be checked before travel.
  • Some medicines may be controlled or restricted.
  • Documentation should match the traveler and prescription.
  • Original packaging can help reduce confusion.
  • Official sources are safer than informal advice.
