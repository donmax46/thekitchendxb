LABELS - CANNABIS - TRAVEL SAFETY

How Can Travelers Identify Cannabis-Related Products Before Dubai Travel?

Travelers should review labels, ingredients and product claims carefully before Dubai travel and avoid anything cannabis-related that cannot be verified.

Read Labels Carefully

Words such as cannabis, hemp, CBD, THC, full-spectrum or cannabinoid-related claims should prompt extra caution before travel.

Products can appear in oils, creams, foods, drinks, supplements and vape liquids.

Avoid Unclear Products

If a traveler cannot clearly verify a product and its legal status for UAE travel, the safest decision is not to pack it.

This guidance is awareness-focused and does not approve or classify products.

Key Takeaways

  • Cannabis-related wording should prompt caution.
  • Cannabis-derived ingredients can appear in many product types.
  • Labels do not guarantee UAE compliance.
  • Unclear products should not be packed.
  • Official verification is essential.
