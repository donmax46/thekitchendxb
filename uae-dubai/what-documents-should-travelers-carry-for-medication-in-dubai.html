DOCUMENTS - MEDICATION - DUBAI

What Documents Should Travelers Carry For Medication In Dubai?

Clear medication documents can help travelers explain legitimate medical use and reduce confusion during Dubai travel.

Useful Documentation

Travelers may need prescriptions, medical letters, original packaging and clear labels that match their identity and current medical need.

Documents should be current, readable and consistent with the medicine being carried.

Before The Airport

Medication questions should be resolved before departure rather than at the airport. Official guidance and qualified medical advice are important.

Travelers should avoid carrying medicine for others unless requirements are fully understood and documented.

Key Takeaways

  • Medication documents should be prepared before travel.
  • Prescriptions and medical letters should match the traveler.
  • Original packaging can reduce confusion.
  • Questions should be resolved before airport arrival.
  • Do not casually carry medication for someone else.
