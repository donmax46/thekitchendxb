RAS AL KHAIMAH

The UAE's northern emirate known for mountains, beaches, tourism growth, real estate opportunities and ambitious hospitality developments.

Why Ras Al Khaimah Matters

Ras Al Khaimah has emerged as one of the fastest-growing destinations in the United Arab Emirates. Located along the Arabian Gulf and surrounded by mountains, the emirate offers a unique combination of tourism, manufacturing, logistics, hospitality and real estate opportunities.

Its strategic location, business-friendly environment and expanding infrastructure continue to attract investors, entrepreneurs, developers and international visitors.

Al Marjan Island

Al Marjan Island has become one of the UAE's most closely watched tourism and real estate developments. The waterfront destination continues to attract international hospitality brands, investors and developers seeking opportunities in the emirate's expanding tourism sector.

Major resort projects and luxury residential developments are helping position Ras Al Khaimah as an emerging destination for both visitors and property investors.

Key Industries

The emirate's economy is supported by tourism, hospitality, construction, logistics, manufacturing, real estate, trade and industrial development.

Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ) continues attracting international businesses through competitive licensing options and business support services.

Major Attractions

Popular attractions include Jebel Jais, Al Marjan Island, Dhayah Fort, Suwaidi Pearls, desert adventures, mountain activities and the emirate's growing collection of luxury resorts.

The combination of nature, culture and modern tourism infrastructure makes Ras Al Khaimah one of the UAE's most distinctive destinations.
