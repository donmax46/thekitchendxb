JOHANNESBURG

South Africa's largest city and Africa's leading financial center, connecting business, investment, entrepreneurship and innovation across the continent.

Why Johannesburg Matters

Johannesburg is the economic heart of South Africa and one of the most influential business cities in Africa. The city serves as a gateway to Southern Africa and hosts major banks, multinational corporations, investment firms and regional headquarters.

Known as the City of Gold, Johannesburg continues to attract entrepreneurs, professionals, investors and global businesses seeking opportunities across the African continent.

Africa's Financial Capital

Johannesburg is home to the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE), Africa's largest stock exchange. The city plays a central role in banking, insurance, investment management and corporate finance across the continent.

Many multinational companies choose Johannesburg as their African headquarters due to its advanced infrastructure, financial markets and access to regional economies.

Key Industries

Major sectors include finance, mining, technology, telecommunications, manufacturing, logistics, professional services, retail and real estate.

Johannesburg remains one of Africa's most diversified economies and a major driver of regional economic growth.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Sandton, Maboneng Precinct, Constitution Hill, Apartheid Museum, Gold Reef City, Johannesburg Botanical Gardens and the city's vibrant arts and cultural scene.

The city offers a blend of business opportunity, cultural diversity, entrepreneurship and modern urban living.
