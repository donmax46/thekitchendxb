MACAO

One of Asia's leading tourism and hospitality destinations, known for luxury resorts, entertainment, culture and international visitors.

Why Macao Matters

Macao is one of the world's most recognized tourism destinations and a major center for hospitality, entertainment and luxury travel. Located on China's southern coast near Hong Kong, the city serves as an important gateway within the Greater Bay Area.

Its blend of Portuguese heritage, Chinese culture, luxury developments and international tourism creates a unique environment unlike any other city in Asia.

Global Tourism Destination

Macao attracts millions of visitors every year and is internationally recognized for its hospitality industry, luxury resorts and entertainment sector.

Its strategic location near Hong Kong, Shenzhen and Guangzhou strengthens its role within one of the world's most economically active regions.

Key Industries

Major sectors include tourism, hospitality, retail, entertainment, food services, finance, transportation and professional services.

Economic diversification initiatives continue encouraging growth in technology, culture, events and international business activities.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Senado Square, Ruins of St. Paul's, Macau Tower, Taipa Village, Coloane, Cotai Strip and numerous luxury integrated resorts.

The city's UNESCO-listed historic center highlights centuries of cultural exchange between Europe and Asia.

Future Growth

Macao continues expanding its tourism offerings while investing in culture, conventions, entertainment and regional business integration.

Its position within the Greater Bay Area supports long-term opportunities in tourism, hospitality and international commerce.
