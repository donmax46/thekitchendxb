TEHRAN

Iran's capital and largest city, serving as the country's political, economic, technological and cultural center.

Why Tehran Matters

Tehran is the heart of Iran's economy and home to millions of residents, universities, businesses and research institutions. The city plays a central role in industry, technology, finance and entrepreneurship across the country.

Located at the foot of the Alborz Mountains, Tehran combines modern urban development with deep historical and cultural roots that span thousands of years.

A Center Of Knowledge And Innovation

Tehran hosts many of Iran's leading universities, engineering schools and research centers. The city has developed strong capabilities in science, technology, manufacturing and engineering.

Its educated workforce and industrial base continue to support entrepreneurship and innovation across multiple sectors.

Key Industries

Major sectors include manufacturing, automotive production, engineering, technology, telecommunications, healthcare, energy services, finance and education.

Tehran remains the primary commercial center for one of the largest economies in the Middle East.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Golestan Palace, Azadi Tower, Milad Tower, Grand Bazaar, Sa'dabad Complex, National Museum of Iran and the scenic mountain districts north of the city.

The city offers a blend of Persian history, modern urban life, cultural institutions and mountain landscapes.

Future Outlook

Continued investment in education, technology, infrastructure and industrial development is helping shape Tehran's future as a center for innovation and economic activity.

Its strategic location and skilled workforce remain important assets for long-term growth.
