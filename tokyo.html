TOKYO

One of the world's most advanced cities, known for technology, finance, innovation, culture and global influence.

Why Tokyo Matters

Tokyo is the capital of Japan and one of the largest metropolitan economies in the world. The city is a global center for technology, finance, manufacturing, research and innovation.

With world-class infrastructure, advanced transportation systems and a highly skilled workforce, Tokyo continues to attract entrepreneurs, investors, multinational corporations and professionals from around the world.

Global Innovation Leader

Tokyo has long been recognized as one of the world's leading centers for innovation. The city is home to major technology companies, research institutions and some of the most advanced manufacturing capabilities on the planet.

Its influence extends across electronics, automotive engineering, robotics, artificial intelligence and digital transformation.

Key Industries

Major sectors include technology, finance, automotive manufacturing, robotics, telecommunications, healthcare, logistics, media and professional services.

Tokyo's economy remains one of the most diversified and resilient in the world.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include Shibuya Crossing, Tokyo Tower, Tokyo Skytree, Asakusa, Meiji Shrine, Akihabara, Ginza, Shinjuku and the Imperial Palace.

Tokyo offers a unique blend of futuristic technology, historic traditions, world-class dining and vibrant cultural experiences.

Future Growth

Tokyo continues investing heavily in artificial intelligence, clean technology, smart infrastructure, advanced transportation and scientific research.

The city's commitment to innovation ensures its position as one of the world's most influential urban centers for decades to come.
