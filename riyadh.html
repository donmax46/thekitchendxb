RIYADH

The capital of Saudi Arabia and one of the fastest-growing business, investment and innovation hubs in the Middle East.

Why Riyadh Matters

Riyadh has become one of the most important economic and political centers in the Middle East. As the capital of Saudi Arabia, the city is at the heart of the Kingdom's Vision 2030 transformation strategy, attracting international businesses, investors and entrepreneurs.

Massive infrastructure projects, economic diversification initiatives and global investment programs continue to reshape Riyadh into a leading destination for business, technology and innovation.

Key Industries

Riyadh's economy is expanding rapidly across finance, technology, construction, logistics, tourism, healthcare, renewable energy and advanced manufacturing.

Government-backed initiatives are encouraging global companies to establish regional headquarters in Riyadh, helping transform the city into a major international business destination.

Major Attractions

Visitors and residents enjoy destinations including Kingdom Centre Tower, Boulevard Riyadh City, Diriyah, King Abdullah Financial District, National Museum of Saudi Arabia and the rapidly expanding entertainment districts across the city.

The city combines Saudi heritage with modern urban development, creating a unique environment for business, tourism and cultural experiences.

Vision 2030 And Future Growth

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 strategy is driving significant investment into Riyadh through infrastructure development, tourism expansion, digital transformation and private sector growth.

Large-scale projects continue to position Riyadh as one of the world's most ambitious urban development programs and a major center for future economic growth.
