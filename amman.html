AMMAN

Jordan's capital city and one of the Middle East's leading centers for entrepreneurship, culture, tourism and regional business.

Why Amman Matters

Amman is the political, economic and cultural heart of Jordan. Located at the crossroads of the Middle East, the city has developed into a regional center for business, education, innovation and diplomacy.

Its stability, skilled workforce and strategic location make it an attractive destination for entrepreneurs, investors and international organizations.

A Regional Business Hub

Amman serves as a gateway to the Levant and wider Middle East. The city hosts international companies, financial institutions, development organizations and a growing startup ecosystem.

Its business-friendly environment continues attracting entrepreneurs and professionals from across the region.

Key Industries

Major sectors include technology, finance, education, healthcare, tourism, telecommunications, professional services, logistics and retail.

Jordan's growing innovation ecosystem has positioned Amman as one of the region's emerging startup capitals.

Major Attractions

Popular destinations include the Amman Citadel, Roman Theatre, Rainbow Street, Abdali Boulevard, King Abdullah I Mosque and the city's historic downtown districts.

Amman also serves as a gateway to Petra, Wadi Rum, Aqaba and the Dead Sea.

Quality Of Life

Amman offers a blend of modern urban living, cultural heritage and regional connectivity. The city is known for its hospitality, food culture and growing international community.

Its balance of opportunity and lifestyle continues attracting residents from around the world.

Future Outlook

Investment in technology, infrastructure, tourism and entrepreneurship continues strengthening Amman's position as a regional center for innovation and business.

Its strategic location and educated workforce support long-term economic growth and international relevance.
