LAGOS

Nigeria's commercial capital and one of Africa's most influential cities for business, finance, technology, entertainment and entrepreneurship.

Why Lagos Matters

Lagos is the economic engine of Nigeria and one of the largest metropolitan areas in Africa. The city serves as a gateway to West Africa, attracting entrepreneurs, investors, multinational corporations and innovators from across the continent.

With a population exceeding 20 million people, Lagos is a major center for trade, finance, logistics, technology, media and creative industries.

Africa's Startup Capital

Lagos has become one of Africa's leading technology ecosystems. The city hosts a growing number of fintech, e-commerce, logistics, digital media and software companies attracting international investment.

Many of Africa's most successful startups launched or established major operations in Lagos due to its large market, entrepreneurial culture and access to talent.

Key Industries

Major sectors include finance, banking, trade, logistics, shipping, technology, telecommunications, entertainment, real estate and manufacturing.

The city's strategic location on the Gulf of Guinea makes Lagos one of Africa's most important commercial and logistics centers.

Major Attractions

Visitors enjoy destinations including Victoria Island, Lekki, Ikoyi, Landmark Beach, Nike Art Gallery, Freedom Park and the city's vibrant food, music and nightlife scene.

Lagos combines modern business districts, coastal living, cultural diversity and entrepreneurial energy unlike any other city in Africa.
